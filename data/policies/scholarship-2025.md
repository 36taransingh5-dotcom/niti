# National Merit Support Scholarship — Scheme Guidelines (2025 Revision)

> **Demonstration Policy — Synthetic / Fictional.** This document is a
> realistic but entirely fictional policy created for the NITI demonstration.
> It does not represent any actual government scheme.

## 1. Purpose

The National Merit Support Scholarship provides financial assistance to
students from lower-income households who are pursuing higher education at
accredited institutions, with the objective of reducing financial barriers
to continued study.

## 2. Eligibility

### 2.1 Age

Applicants must have attained the age of 18 years on or before the date of
application.

### 2.2 Enrolment

The applicant shall be enrolled, at the time of application, in an
institution accredited by the competent authority.

### 2.3 Course of Study

The scholarship is available to students pursuing full-time undergraduate or
postgraduate degree programmes.

## 3. Financial Criteria

### 3.1 Assessment Basis

Household income shall be assessed on the basis of the income certificate
issued by the competent revenue authority.

### 3.2 Household Income

Applicants with an annual household income not exceeding ₹3,00,000 (Rupees
three lakh) shall be eligible for assistance under this scheme.

## 4. Required Documents

Every application shall be accompanied by proof of identity and proof of
residential address. A household income certificate issued by the competent
revenue authority, not older than twelve months, shall be verified before
disbursal. A certificate of enrolment for the current academic year,
countersigned by the head of the institution, shall be verified during
processing.

## 5. Processing

Applications shall be evaluated against the eligibility criteria set out in
Sections 2 and 3. Applications satisfying the automatic criteria shall
proceed to document verification, followed by review and final decision by
the designated caseworker. Applicants shall be informed of the outcome with
reference to the specific provisions applied.
